      <span class="section-eyebrow">Pack shape</span>
      <h1 class="page-title mb-3">What's in the pack</h1>
      <p class="page-lead mb-0"><span data-mod-count>128</span> mods, organised by what they let you do rather than what they are called. Here is the shape of the thing.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="row g-4 mb-4">
    <div class="col-lg-6" id="automate">
      <div class="card thunder-card h-100">
        <div class="card-body">
          <span class="section-eyebrow">Tech</span>
          <h2 class="section-title mb-3">Automate everything</h2>
          <p class="card-text">If you have ever looked at a repetitive job and thought "there must be a better way", Thunder probably gives you three.</p>
          <ul class="list-copy mb-0">
            <li><strong>Create</strong> gives you belts, shafts, cogs, presses, trains, and contraptions that are just plain fun to watch once they start moving.</li>
            <li><strong>Mekanism</strong> takes things into heavier industry with ore processing, chemicals, power generation, and big machinery that means business.</li>
            <li><strong>Applied Energistics 2</strong>, <strong>Refined Storage</strong>, <strong>Storage Drawers</strong>, and <strong>Modular Routers</strong> help stop your base becoming a museum of badly labelled chests.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="col-lg-6" id="magic">
      <div class="card thunder-card h-100">
        <div class="card-body">
          <span class="section-eyebrow">Magic</span>
          <h2 class="section-title mb-3">Spells, rituals and witchcraft</h2>
          <p class="card-text">These are not three mods that all do the same trick in different hats. Each one has its own flavour.</p>
          <ul class="list-copy mb-0">
            <li><strong>Ars Nouveau</strong> lets you build your own spells from glyphs, which means plenty of room for both clever utility and bad ideas.</li>
            <li><strong>Blood Magic</strong> is slower, stranger, and far more ritual-heavy if you fancy power with a bit of menace to it.</li>
            <li><strong>Hexerei</strong> brings brooms, potions, familiars, and enough atmosphere to make a forest cottage feel genuinely enchanted.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="col-lg-6" id="build">
      <div class="card thunder-card h-100">
        <div class="card-body">
          <span class="section-eyebrow">Building</span>
          <h2 class="section-title mb-3">Build something worth showing off</h2>
          <p class="card-text">Vanilla gives you the basics. Thunder gives you room to make a base that does not look like a hurried bunker.</p>
          <ul class="list-copy mb-0">
            <li><strong>Macaw's suite</strong> adds doors, roofs, bridges, windows, fences, and all the bits that make builds feel finished.</li>
            <li><strong>Chipped</strong> and <strong>Rechiseled</strong> add a ridiculous number of block variants when you want a palette finer than "stone, but slightly different".</li>
            <li><strong>Create Deco</strong> and related pieces help industrial builds look clean, deliberate, and properly joined up.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="col-lg-6" id="explore">
      <div class="card thunder-card h-100">
        <div class="card-body">
          <span class="section-eyebrow">Exploration</span>
          <h2 class="section-title mb-3">Wander off and find something interesting</h2>
          <p class="card-text">The Overworld should feel worth poking about in rather than just something you sprint through on the way to the next machine.</p>
          <ul class="list-copy mb-0">
            <li><strong>Biomes O' Plenty</strong> makes the world far less samey and gives different regions a proper identity.</li>
            <li><strong>Alex's Mobs</strong> adds more life, more oddities, and the occasional thing you would rather not stumble into underground.</li>
            <li><strong>Oh The Trees You'll Grow</strong> and <strong>Nature's Compass</strong> make exploration more varied and less of a blind trudge.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="col-lg-6" id="qol">
      <div class="card thunder-card h-100">
        <div class="card-body">
          <span class="section-eyebrow">Daily life</span>
          <h2 class="section-title mb-3">Less faff, better multiplayer</h2>
          <p class="card-text">A big modpack needs the boring little improvements as much as the headline toys.</p>
          <ul class="list-copy mb-0">
            <li><strong>JEI</strong> and <strong>Jade</strong> keep the pack readable instead of leaving you to guess recipes and block behaviour.</li>
            <li><strong>Waystones</strong>, <strong>Xaero's maps</strong>, <strong>Sophisticated Backpacks</strong>, and <strong>Lootr</strong> make ordinary play much less tedious.</li>
            <li><strong>Simple Voice Chat</strong> means people can just talk in-game instead of vanishing into another app every five minutes.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="col-lg-6" id="server">
      <div class="card thunder-card h-100">
        <div class="card-body">
          <span class="section-eyebrow">Hosting</span>
          <h2 class="section-title mb-3">Server support that is actually useful</h2>
          <p class="card-text">Running the pack should not require a second hobby in deployment archaeology.</p>
          <ul class="list-copy mb-0">
            <li>Thunder ships install and startup scripts for Linux and Windows, plus a <code>pterodactyl.json</code> egg for panel hosting.</li>
            <li><strong>LuckPerms</strong>, <strong>FTB Essentials</strong>, <strong>WorldEdit</strong>, <strong>SecurityCraft</strong>, and <strong>GriefLogger</strong> cover the usual admin jobs.</li>
            <li>The packwiz metadata host keeps server sync simple instead of leaving you to manually rebuild the pack from scraps.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>
</div>
